FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Common questions and short answers.

  1. 01

    What should I bring to my session?

    Towels, a robe and slippers are provided; you only need to bring your personal items.

  2. 02

    Is massage or the hammam suitable for my health?

    If you are pregnant, have a chronic condition or recently had surgery, please consult your doctor before the session. Spa services are for relaxation and are not a substitute for medical treatment.

  3. 03

    Are the images and data on the site real?

    No; this is a setup example. Images are representative and names, figures and people are fictional.

  4. 04

    How do I find out the prices?

    Prices depend on the treatment and its length and are confirmed in writing when you book; this demo text shows no prices.
